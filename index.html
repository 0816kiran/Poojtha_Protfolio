A little about my approach

Creative ideas, thoughtful details.

I’m Poojtha, a freelance video editor and visual designer working across digital content and brand visuals. I enjoy turning a brief into clear, engaging creative work—whether that means shaping the rhythm of a video, building a product-focused design, or giving a brand a consistent visual direction.
